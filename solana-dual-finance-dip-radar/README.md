# Solana Dual Finance Staking Options & Dual Investment Product (DIP) Radar

Automated quantitative analytics and strike optimization engine for Dual Finance Dual Investment Products (Sell-High / Buy-Low DIP), Staking Options (SO) intrinsic valuation, and treasury dilution mitigation on Solana.

## Key Capabilities
- **Dual Investment Products (DIP) Outcomes:** Calculates guaranteed yield amounts and settlement currency payoffs across covered calls ("Sell-High") and cash-secured puts ("Buy-Low") ($52.14\%\text{ APR} \to 1.0\%\text{ net return in }7\text{ days}$).
- **Staking Options (SO) Fair Value:** Models Black-Scholes intrinsic values for DAO incentive grants and tracks treasury cash inflows upon contributor option exercise ($20,000\text{ USDC treasury inflow}$ on $10,000\text{ options}$).
- **Optimal DIP Strike Risk Profiler:** Ranks available strike ladders by APR and buffer distance from spot price to maximize risk-adjusted yields.
- **VIP Real-Yield License:** Verifies 5 Base USDC or 0.03 SOL on-chain payment for automated webhook alerts.
