# Base Superform Universal Cross-Chain Vault & Yield Intent Radar

Automated quantitative analytics and execution optimization engine for Superform Protocol ERC-4626 multi-chain yield vaults, SuperPosition (ERC-1155) secondary market basis arbitrage, and multicall deposit batching on Base.

## Key Capabilities
- **Cross-Chain Vault Carry & Fee Amortization:** Calculates net realized yield across ERC-4626 vaults after cross-chain relayer fees, Base L2 gas, and protocol routing fees, determining exact breakeven holding periods (e.g. 3.41 days on $10,000 at 12% APY).
- **Multicall Deposit Batching Efficiency:** Quantifies gas reductions achieved by bundling multiple cross-chain vault deposits into single Superform UserOps (57.78% gas savings across 5 vaults).
- **SuperPosition Secondary Basis Arbitrage:** Models secondary market purchase discounts (25 bps) against native redemption for instantaneous annualized yields (>2,000% IRR on 1-hour settlement).
- **VIP Commercial Licensing:** Integrated on-chain license verification for 5 Base USDC or 0.03 SOL.
