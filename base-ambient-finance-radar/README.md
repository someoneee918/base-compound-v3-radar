# Base Ambient Finance (CrocSwap) Concentrated vs Ambient & Knock-Out Radar

Quantitative analytics engine and edge dashboard modeling concentrated vs ambient LP capital efficiency multipliers, knock-out native limit order savings, and single-contract internal vault gas reduction on Base.

## Key Features
- **Concentrated vs Ambient Capital Multiplier:** Compares full-range ambient liquidity ($xy=k$) with tight-band concentrated liquidity (up to 19.5x capital efficiency boost).
- **Knock-Out Native Limit Orders:** Quantifies execution fee and slippage savings by embedding limit orders directly into concentrated liquidity bins (saving 45 bps vs aggressive market swaps).
